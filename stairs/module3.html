MODULE 3 / REVIEW DRAFT 3SCOPE

Finish the framing
Module 3 · steps 7–9 (3D)

Review draft · 4 November 2026. Static shop instructions for the finalized 20-inch-wide, nine-step model. Module 3 spans 30 degrees and contains global steps 7, 8 and 9. Its wooden foundation is already complete; casters are NOT installed until the very end.

Included

43 wooden pieces and 10 purchased connectors. Treads, uprights, step framing and risers, selected by the model's visibility categories. Spindle backing and its flanges are included because the model classifies them as step framing.

Not included: foundation construction, other modules' construction, siding, ribs, spindles, rails or intermodule joining. Tread spindle notches are deliberately deferred.

Read before beginning

Build all three step frames and their uprights OFF the foundation first. Temporary P is the level plane under the six full-length upright bottoms. After transfer, P is the TOP of the foundation cross-pieces, which sit ON TOP of the long beams. The same height measurements apply in both positions; do not measure from an uneven floor or add caster height. INNER/left is the small-radius side facing uphill; FRONT is downhill. P-xxx identifies a unique cutting part across the project; repeated labels identify separate pieces of that part.

Review draft: model geometry is fixed, but wood-joint screws and the actual flange thread pitch are not specified. Specify joint fasteners and verify purchased hardware before permanent fastening or drilling bolt holes. This draft does not replace those missing specifications.

Tools: tape/ruler, square, pencil, level, clamps, stable trestles and diagonal battens; miter saw with bevel capability, suitable rip/plywood cutting tools, drill and countersink; trammel for arcs. Arrange help for the braced-carcass transfers. Use long stock/carriers for small wedges.
