MODULE 2 / REVIEW DRAFT 3SCOPE

Finish the framing
Module 2 · steps 4–6

Third review iteration · 3 November 2026. Static shop instructions for the finalized 20-inch-wide, nine-step model. Module 2 spans 30 degrees and contains global steps 4, 5 and 6. Wooden foundations for modules 2 and 3 exist; casters are NOT installed until the very end.

Included

45 wooden pieces and 14 purchased connectors. Treads, uprights, step framing and risers, selected by the model's visibility categories. Spindle backing and its flanges are included because the model classifies them as step framing.

Not included: foundation construction, module 1 or 3 construction, siding, ribs, spindles, rails or intermodule joining. Tread spindle notches are deliberately deferred.

Read before beginning

Build all three step frames and their uprights OFF the foundation first. Temporary P is the level plane under the six full-length upright bottoms. After transfer, P is the TOP of the foundation cross-pieces, which sit ON TOP of the long beams. The same height measurements apply in both positions; do not measure from an uneven floor or add caster height. INNER/left is the small-radius side facing uphill; FRONT is downhill. Diagram numbers abbreviate M2-xx.

Review draft: model geometry is fixed, but wood-joint screws and the actual flange thread pitch are not specified. Complete the fastening worksheet and verify purchased hardware before permanent fastening or drilling bolt holes. This draft does not replace those missing specifications.

Tools: tape/ruler, square, pencil, level, clamps, stable trestles and diagonal battens; miter saw with bevel capability, suitable rip/plywood cutting tools, drill and countersink; trammel for arcs. Arrange help for the braced-carcass transfers. Use long stock/carriers for small wedges.

MODULE 2 / REVIEW DRAFT 3TREAD TEMPLATES / DATUMS

Trace the cut treads; transfer post marks

Cut the unnotched plywood treads FIRST. Use their actual edges to make the full-size placement pattern; no large-radius construction layout is needed once the treads are cut. This printed picture is reduced, not a full-size template.

  1. Lay M2-19, M2-36, M2-53 TOP up, with consecutive rear/front radial edges touching and both curves continuous. Trace them on joined paper. Mark each seam, step number, FRONT edge and INNER curve. Do not turn a tread upside down or mirror a tracing.
  2. At each tread's front-inner ARC corner, use its straight FRONT edge as the ruler baseline u, increasing outward. Square a line uphill, perpendicular to that edge: v. Use the post-strip table to mark the upright corners on this FLAT tracing. Connect each set of four; a 3/4 x 3 1/2 offcut checks the front post-strip size.
  3. Draw a parallel line 1/4 behind each tread FRONT edge. Label it CROSS-PIECE FRONT. Cut three sides of each paper upright rectangle so it folds open as a marking window; do not cut these windows in the plywood. Keep the seams between steps fixed with tape.
  4. Transfer the post windows to a level staging/layout board for the OFF-foundation build. The six full-length post bottoms share this temporary P plane; the two short left posts sit on the long rail. Use plumb lines to transfer their paper marks up to that rail.
  5. After all three frames and uprights are assembled, reuse the paper on the level, caster-free foundation. Align all three CROSS-PIECE FRONT lines to the actual front cross-piece edges, then transfer the windows. Remove the paper before dry-seating the complete carcass. Do not fasten lower blocks in advance.

The level staging plane represents the future cross-piece tops P, not the finished floor. No caster allowance is added. If the pattern and foundation do not register together, resolve the mismatch before fastening. The inner template corner is on the ARC, not the model's trapezoidal footprint corner.

MODULE 2 / REVIEW DRAFT 3ASSEMBLY

1. Cut the treads first; make placement templates

  1. Start with the three unnotched plywood treads M2-19, M2-36, M2-53: draw them using the dimensioned tread layout (page 45). Label TOP, FRONT, INNER and step number. Do not cut spindle notches or attach treads permanently yet.
  2. With TOP faces up on a flat surface, place step 4's REAR radial edge against step 5's FRONT edge, then step 5's rear against step 6's front. The inner and outer arcs must continue smoothly. Trace all three on paper; keep their seam lines and orientation labels.
  3. Make the reusable paper placement pattern as described on the template-layout page. Mark upright footprints using the post-strip sheet. Paper windows let you transfer marks to the foundation without cutting or drilling the plywood templates.
  4. Cut and label the remaining wooden parts. Mark stock start, near edge and A/B faces before making bevels or notches. Prepare a level layout board/support plane for the six full-length upright bottoms. This temporary plane represents P; after transfer, P is the foundation cross-piece TOP. Do not fasten any upright or lower block to the foundation yet.
  5. Dry-fit joints; clearance-drill the first wooden member and pilot the receiving member, using the screw maker's sizes and the agreed fastener schedule. Keep holes clear of half-lap shoulders and future bolt centers. Do not glue until the complete local dry-fit checks out.

Screw gauge, lengths, quantities, edge distances and pilot diameters are not specified by the model. Record these in the fastening worksheet before permanent assembly. The numbered pieces and geometry are specified here; the fastening schedule remains a review item.

3. Build the bottom step frame first

Exploded view: boards retain their installed orientation; gaps are illustrative, not measurements. Dotted lines connect mating joint locations. M2-11/12 have TOP cutouts; M2-13/14 have BOTTOM cutouts. The rear-left M2-12 / M2-14 connection is a BUTT joint, not a fourth half-lap. All top edges are flush when assembled.

  1. On a flat layout board, set M2-11, M2-12, M2-13, M2-14 edge-on with their lower edges supported. Dry-fit to the step-4 plan (page 9). The long M2-14 continues past this tread to the rear cross-member; do NOT shorten it to the tread outline.

  3. Dry-lay tread M2-19 to check the curved outline and front registration, then remove it. Let the frame joints set before raising the frame. Do not add a tread, riser or lower brace yet.
  4. Leave M2-48 separate while this frame rests on the layout board. After raising and supporting the frame in the next operation, fit M2-48 between BOTH rear uprights M2-04, M2-08, UNDER the rear end of M2-14.
