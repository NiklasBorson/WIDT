MODULE 2 / REVIEW DRAFT 3SCOPE

Finish the framing
Module 2 · steps 4–6

Third review iteration · 3 November 2026. Static shop instructions for the finalized 20-inch-wide, nine-step model. Module 2 spans 30 degrees and contains global steps 4, 5 and 6. Wooden foundations for modules 2 and 3 exist; casters are NOT installed until the very end.

Included

45 wooden pieces and 14 purchased connectors. Treads, uprights, step framing and risers, selected by the model's visibility categories. Spindle backing and its flanges are included because the model classifies them as step framing.

Not included: foundation construction, module 1 or 3 construction, siding, ribs, spindles, rails or intermodule joining. Tread spindle notches are deliberately deferred.

Read before beginning

Build all three step frames and their uprights OFF the foundation first. Temporary P is the level plane under the six full-length upright bottoms. After transfer, P is the TOP of the foundation cross-pieces, which sit ON TOP of the long beams. The same height measurements apply in both positions; do not measure from an uneven floor or add caster height. INNER/left is the small-radius side facing uphill; FRONT is downhill. Diagram numbers abbreviate M2-xx.

Review draft: model geometry is fixed, but wood-joint screws and the actual flange thread pitch are not specified. Complete the fastening worksheet and verify purchased hardware before permanent fastening or drilling bolt holes. This draft does not replace those missing specifications.

Tools: tape/ruler, square, pencil, level, clamps, stable trestles and diagonal battens; miter saw with bevel capability, suitable rip/plywood cutting tools, drill and countersink; trammel for arcs. Arrange help for the braced-carcass transfers. Use long stock/carriers for small wedges.

M2-19, M2-36, M2-53 · curved plywood treads

Make 3 from 1/2-inch plywood; identical unnotched outlines. Blank: ~10 3/16 along grain × ~20 1/8 across grain.

Solid outline = cutting lines. Dashed straight chords are measuring checks, NOT cutting lines or arc lengths. Inches rounded to 1/16; ~ = approximate. Shared center C lies outside the plywood.

Concentric arcs; 10 degrees between radial end cuts. All cuts square through the thickness. Spindle notches deferred. Not a full-size template.
