Build the foundation and framing
Module 1 · steps 1–3

Review draft · 4 November 2026. Static shop instructions for the finalized 20-inch-wide, nine-step model. Module 1 spans 30 degrees and contains global steps 1, 2 and 3. Build its wooden foundation as part of this document; casters are NOT installed until the very end.

Included

46 wooden pieces and 4 purchased connectors. Treads, uprights, step framing and risers, plus the wooden foundation, newel post, its mounting blocks and both front-spindle supports. These are included even where the model classifies them as banister parts.

Not included: other modules' foundations or framing, siding, ribs, spindles, rails or intermodule joining. Tread spindle notches are deliberately deferred.

Read before beginning

Build the foundation first, then fit the upper framing to it. P is the level TOP of the step-2 and step-3 cross-pieces, which sit ON TOP of the long beams. All six upright bottoms bear on P. Step 1 has NO uprights or conventional four-board frame; its low supports and apron carry its tread. Support the foundation on level temporary blocking, leaving access below; do not measure from an uneven floor or add caster height. INNER/left is the small-radius side facing uphill; FRONT is downhill. P-xxx identifies a unique cutting part across the project; repeated labels identify separate pieces of that part.

Review draft: model geometry is fixed, but wood-joint screws and the actual flange thread pitch are not specified. Specify joint fasteners and verify purchased hardware before permanent fastening or drilling bolt holes. This draft does not replace those missing specifications.

Tools: tape/ruler, square, pencil, level, clamps, stable trestles and diagonal battens; miter saw with bevel capability, suitable rip/plywood cutting tools, drill and countersink; trammel for arcs. Support and brace the foundation and framing throughout assembly. Use long stock/carriers for small wedges.

1. Cut the treads; use them as placement patterns

Use the actual treads as temporary placement patterns: P-002 for step 1, then P-001 for steps 2 and 3. TOP faces up; adjoining radial edges meet and the INNER curve stays continuous. The first tread has a STRAIGHT outer edge and a newel cutout. See the standard tread layout (cut list, page 1) and first-step tread (cut list, page 2). Keep treads loose and spindle notches uncut.

2. Build the foundation beams

Dark = parts worked on; pale = supporting context. Final orientation, not a lifting trajectory. Temporary supports, diagonal braces and clamps are not shown.

Setting out: both long-beam FRONT ends are 1″ behind the first tread's FRONT edge, flush with the later front apron's REAR face. At that line, measure parallel to the FRONT edge from the tread's FRONT/INNER corner: P-039 INNER corner ~~1 1/16″; P-040 INNER corner ~~12 15/16″.

The two P-041 cross-pieces sit ON TOP of the long beams, each with its FRONT edge 1/4″ behind its step-2 or step-3 tread FRONT edge. The clipped P-042 rear cross-piece fits behind the step-3 cross-piece. Their TOPS define level plane P. Use temporary blocking, not casters.

4. Add the first-step supports and newel blocks

Dark = parts worked on; pale = supporting context. Final orientation, not a lifting trajectory. Temporary supports, diagonal braces and clamps are not shown.

Low first-step stack: P-039, P-040 beam TOPS and P-043, P-015 block TOPS are 1 1/2″ BELOW P. The flat P-022, P-023 bearings add 3/4″; their three on-edge supports add 1 1/2″. Support TOPS and front apron P-037 TOP meet at P + 3/4″, the step-1 tread underside.

The apron FRONT is 1/4″ behind the tread FRONT. The mounting blocks are structural framing; fit the newel in section 7. Check its opening against the actual first tread's cutout; do not bridge that opening with a support.

7. Fit the newel, spindle supports and shims

Dark = parts worked on; pale = supporting context. Final orientation, not a lifting trajectory. Temporary supports, diagonal braces and clamps are not shown.

Set newel P-047 plumb against mounting blocks P-043, P-015; its bevel fits apron P-037. Align the bevel TOP shoulder with the apron TOP; the bevel continues to the post BOTTOM, with no lower shoulder. Face A faces INWARD toward the stairs; the NEAR-edge face faces FRONT/downhill. Screw the mounting blocks to the post from INSIDE the open frame before fitting any riser or tread. Use the actual step-1 tread only as a loose clearance check, then remove it.

Step 2 has a short, wide P-044 support above its reduced-height lower attachment block. Step 3 has the longer P-013 support. Fit each tapered bearing shim in its actual source position; module 1 does NOT use the upper modules' backing-block/flange arrangement here.
