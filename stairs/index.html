Curved stairway

Bottom frames arranged through 90 degrees, each with two side boards, two vertical uprights, and a 1x4 cross-piece between the uprights. The final step has two extra uprights at the far radial boundary, with the rear-left upright narrowed if needed to clear the front one. Top frames have two side boards ending at the back boundary and a cross-piece at each end supporting a half-inch-thick tread flush with the side and back edges, extending forward to the front of the riser. Separate front risers reach from the previous step's elevation to half an inch below each step's top, with one inch of floor clearance under the bottom step's riser.
