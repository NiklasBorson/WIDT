Planning assumptions

  • Lumber: compare standard 8-, 10-, and 12-ft boards and mixes of those lengths, reserving 2 in at each end for trimming and defects. Confirm local availability for the species and cross section before choosing an option.
  • Sheet goods: standard 4 x 8-ft sheets, reserving 1/2 in at every edge. Use 1/8-in plywood suitable for the curved side panels and confirm its bending direction.
  • Add 1/2 in to each blank length (and each sheet blank width), round up to the next 1/4 in, and allow a 1/8-in saw kerf.
  • Simple long-first board cutting and rectangular sheet rows, not minimum-waste optimization. No individual sheet-blank rotations, irregular nesting, shared rip strips, or reuse of cutouts. Actual store dimensions must meet the stated sizes.
  • Quantities include those cutting allowances, but not extra whole boards or sheets for major mistakes. Reusable leftovers are expected. Check existing offcuts before buying whole boards for small groups.

Wood references: Eastern white pine and Douglas fir. Lumber sizes below are nominal; actual cross sections are shown underneath.
